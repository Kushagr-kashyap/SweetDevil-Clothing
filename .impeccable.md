# Sweet Devil — Design Context

## Users

18–35, internet-culture and alt/metal-music youth in India. They browse on a **phone**, usually at night, usually from an Instagram or Reddit link. They are buying identity, not fabric — an oversized black graphic tee is a statement they wear to a gig, a college fest, or a night out. They have seen a thousand Shopify stores and are bored of all of them.

The job to be done: *"show me something my friends don't have yet, and make me feel something while I look at it."*

## Brand Personality

**Loud. Mechanical. Unholy.**

Irreverent, a little blasphemous, never earnest. Sweet Devil is in on the joke — Brainrot Club sits next to Devil's Club without irony collapse. The voice is short, declarative, and slightly hostile. Never corporate, never apologetic, never "shop our curated collection."

Tagline: *unleash your inner devil.*

## Aesthetic Direction

**Concept: cursed firmware.** The website is a piece of possessed hardware — a machine with a demon booted into it. Everything is framed like equipment: chamfered chassis panels, hairline strokes, system readouts, SKU codes, temperature telemetry. Then it runs hot: fire, ember glow, blood red.

- **Theme: dark.** Non-negotiable and derived, not decorative — night browsing, band-tee culture, black garments photographed on black.
- **Reference:** ray-ban.com/l/discover-ray-ban-exe (chamfered frame, `.EXE` wordmark, heavy grotesque titles, thumbnail strip, full-width chamfered CTA). Also string-tune.fiddle.digital and smooothy.federic.ooo for kinetic drag feel.
- **Anti-references:** generic Shopify/Wix tee stores. Purple-and-cyan "AI cyberpunk." Anything soft, rounded, pastel, or friendly. Glassmorphism as decoration.

### Palette

Derived from the brand's own assets, not invented. `#8B0000` is the live Wix site's red; `#FF5757` is the mascot's body color; the orange comes from `fire.mp4`.

- `--void` — near-black, red-tinted, page base
- `--blood` — `#8B0000`, the brand red, structural
- `--ember` — hot red, the 10% accent
- `--flare` — fire orange, used sparingly for heat
- `--bone` — warm off-white, all body text

Never pure black or pure white. Neutrals are tinted toward hue 25–30 so the chassis and the fire feel like one object. **No cyan. No purple.**

### Typography

- **Display:** Archivo Black — heavy industrial grotesque, matches the equipment-labeling feel and the reference's title weight.
- **Body/UI:** Archivo — same superfamily, wide weight range, stays legible at mobile sizes.
- **System readouts:** Azeret Mono — used *only* for things a machine would actually print (SKU, stock counts, telemetry, timestamps). Never as generic "techy" decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Mobile is the design, not the fallback.** The live Wix site overflows horizontally on a phone. Every layout decision starts at 375px and earns its way up.
2. **The chassis is the brand.** Chamfered corners and hairline strokes are the one thing someone remembers. Apply them systematically — one `--cut` token, never ad-hoc.
3. **Heat is earned.** Ember and flare are ~10% of visual weight. When everything glows, nothing does.
4. **Machine text must be true.** If it's set in mono, it should read like real telemetry tied to real data (actual stock, actual price, actual SKU) — not lorem-ipsum "SYS.READY" noise.
5. **Keep every word.** This is a re-skin of a real store. Products, prices, collections, and the custom-design flow all survive intact.
